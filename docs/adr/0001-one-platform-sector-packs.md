# ADR 0001: One platform, sector packs as separate products

Status: Accepted 10 Nov 2026 (resolution 1)

## Decision

Sector packs (Energy and Oil, Sovereign Capital and Treasury, Banking, and so on) are configurations of one platform: shared ontology, case workflow, governance and memory. Each pack can be named, packaged and priced as its own product, but never has its own codebase or data model.

## Why

Avoids duplicated engines and keeps cross-sector reasoning possible. Packs add ontology extensions, calculators, scenario templates, agent profiles, policies and connector mappings.
