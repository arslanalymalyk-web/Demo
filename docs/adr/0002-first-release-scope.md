# ADR 0002: First release: core plus two packs

Status: Accepted (resolution 2)

## Decision

About 16 weeks: platform core plus Energy and Oil and Sovereign Capital and Treasury, three scenario templates each. Other packs follow in gated phases.

## Why

Keeps quality and traceability intact. Four-plus packs with eight-plus templates each would need roughly 8 to 10 months.
